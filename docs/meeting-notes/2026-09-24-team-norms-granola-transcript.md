## Summary of agreements

**Communication**
- Primary tool: WhatsApp group chat (already created).
- Reasonable response time: roughly 4 hours during the day, nothing over 8 hours. Messages after ~8–10 PM can be answered the next morning. Monday–Thursday be on top of it; Friday and weekends are more lenient.
- Going silent for 24+ hours means the team will "blow you up" or show up at your room.

**Work expectations**
- Meet outside class as needed; everyone sends their schedules to the group chat. Gabe offered to add his bot to the WhatsApp group to help coordinate.
- Gabe: 8 AM class Mon/Wed/Fri, done at 2:15 PM. Tue/Thu only this class (3:30–4), free before 3 and after 5.
- Tristan: Tue/Thu free before 3 and after 5 (this is his one class those days).
- One member has classes until 8 PM on Tue/Thu and is done at 1 PM on Fridays.
- Advance notice is needed for meetings.
- "Done" means polished best work; once everyone is done, all four review each other's sections to confirm the standard.
- Work is divided by assignment, with people volunteering based on skills. Strengths named: creative/design (Illustrator, Photoshop), writing, AI tooling.
- Missing a deadline is not acceptable barring a real emergency; joked about fantasy-football-style punishments.

**Decision-making**
- Vote when there is disagreement; the person doing the work gets a bit more weight. If it is 2 vs 2, compromise.

**Partner relationship**
- Point of contact with IDEAS for Us: Charlotte.
- Follow-up email after each partner meeting: Charlotte (same person as point of contact).

**Quality and help**
- Review each other's work before submitting: yes, when needed.
- AI policy: use it.
- If struggling, say so as soon as you know, not the day of. Two hours before the deadline is too late.
- If a teammate is not pulling their weight: be direct with each other first. Escalate to the instructor after one or two strikes, not three.
