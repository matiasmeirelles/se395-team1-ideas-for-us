# Individual Reflection: Planning Lab

**Leonardo Takeshi Campi Kiyose** · SE 395-2 · IDEAS For Us

Overall the lab went well. The interview made me rank our features against what the Runway already does, so cutting the Grant Finder was an easy call. One thing I got wrong was ranking the Tracker third even though Clay asked for it first. The council caught that, and our team fixed it. I was also surprised that all four audits flagged the same two features (reviewer edits and outcome insights), which the council had already told us to cut. Some challenges I didn't see coming: we never agreed on how many hours a week each person had, nobody counted Thanksgiving week, and by the time the plan was done we were already in Week 9. Next time I'd settle hours and dates first, and I'd give longer answers in the interview instead of just picking the AI's options.

This was a lot more structured than how I usually plan, which is more informal. The audits caught real problems I would have missed, like an approval still counting after someone changes the grant amount, or a draft looking checked when the AI call actually failed. But the AI never talked to IDEAS, so a few things in the plan are still guesses: whether they want a draft checker or generated drafts, the exact dates, and some default numbers. Our first task (T0) is there to get those answers, but parts of the plan could still change.
