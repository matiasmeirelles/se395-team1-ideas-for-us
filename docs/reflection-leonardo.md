# Individual Reflection: Planning Lab

**Leonardo Takeshi Campi Kiyose** · SE 395-2 · IDEAS For Us

I think the lab went pretty well. The interview helped me see that the Runway already does a lot of what we planned to build, so cutting the Grant Finder made sense. But I messed up by putting the Tracker third when Clay basically told us it was the most important thing. The council pointed that out, and we fixed it as a team. What surprised me was that all four of our audits called out the same two features we kept even after the council said to cut them. I also didn't expect how messy the hours would get. We each assumed a different number of hours per week, nobody thought about Thanksgiving, and by the time we finished the plan we were already in Week 9. Next time I'd figure out hours and dates first, and I'd actually explain my answers in the interview instead of just picking A or B.

This was way more organized than how I normally plan, which is usually just figuring it out as we go. The audits found stuff I never would have thought of, like what happens if someone changes the grant amount after it's already approved, or if the AI fails and a draft looks checked when it really isn't. But the AI never actually talked to IDEAS, so some parts of the plan are still guesses, like whether they even want a draft checker and what the real dates are. That's why our first task is getting those answers from them, but honestly some of the plan might still change.
