# Operations Map: IDEAS For Us

**By Leonardo Takeshi**

## What I Found

IDEAS For Us is a small nonprofit in Orlando that does a lot with very few people. They only have a few paid staff, and most of the real work is done by interns and volunteers. Their main local program is Fleet Farming, where people donate their lawns and the team turns them into small farms. Almost all of Fleet Farming runs out of one place, a church in Audubon Park. They also have branches in more than ten countries, which is a lot for such a small team.

**From my research:**
- Interns don't get paid. They have to go to a weekly meeting and work at least 6 hours a week.
- Staff work four days a week and report to a leadership team.
- Fleet Farming keeps its tools and seedlings at the church, and volunteers bike out from there to the farms. In 2026, the city said they needed a permit to keep using the site. They had moved everything there after their old place got robbed.
- Their website runs on WordPress, and Fleet Farming has its own separate website and email.
- The impact numbers on their homepage all show zero.

## What This Means

The biggest thing I noticed is that a lot depends on just one thing working. Fleet Farming moved to the church to be safer, but now they don't have a backup. If they lost that site, the whole program would stop. The farms are all close to the church, so even moving a little would be a big problem.

The interns are another issue. Since new interns come in every semester, a lot of what they learn probably leaves with them. If nothing is written down, staff have to teach everything again every few months, and that takes time away from growing the programs.

I also think they might have a data problem. They track what they grow, but their website still shows zero impact. That makes me think the information isn't getting to the people who need it, like donors or grant writers. Having two websites and two emails could make that even messier.

Last, I'm curious how they keep track of everything happening in other countries with such a small team. I don't know the answer yet, but I want to find out.

## What I Still Need to Find Out

- How many paid staff do they have, and who is in charge of what?
- What happened with the permit, and do they have a backup place?
- Do interns get a written guide when they start, or do they just learn as they go?
- Where do they keep their farm data, and does anyone use it?
- What apps or software do they use for donors, volunteers, and money?
- How do the branches in other countries stay in touch with Orlando?
- What task takes up the most of their time?
