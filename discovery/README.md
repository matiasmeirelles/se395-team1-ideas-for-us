This folder contains all discovery phase work (Weeks 1-7).

- `2026-09-25-partner-kickoff.md`
- `2026-10-02-stakeholder-interview-jane.md`

Background research, competitor analysis, and domain exploration.

### findings/
Synthesis documents and key insights from your discovery work. This is where you connect the dots from interviews and research into actionable findings.
