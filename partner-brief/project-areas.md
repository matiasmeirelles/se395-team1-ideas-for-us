# IDEAS for Us: Proposed Student Project Areas

## 1. Hardee County Biochar Project

Agricultural project using dead citrus trees (killed by citrus greening disease) to create biochar. Hardee County lost 90%+ of citrus production.

Student team would work on:
- Data curation
- Storytelling
- Grant case-building
- Political network mapping (senators, representatives, governor's office)

Long-term project extending into 2027.

## 2. Claude Corps / Anthropic Partnership

Student invited to participate directly in Cohort 2 (starts November). Working with Anthropic's team on internal AI structures.

- Potential trip to California conference
- High-access, high-visibility opportunity

## 3. Winter Park Urban Sustainability

Focus on the Maitland side of Winter Park (neglected compared to Park Avenue side).

Areas of interest:
- Tree canopy, complete streets, bike trails, pocket parks
- Orlando mayoral power vacuum creating upheaval in city governance
- AI proposed to analyze town hall meeting minutes, voting records, commissioner and committee data
- Goal: accelerate strategic decision-making in an uncertain political environment

## Open to Other Ideas

These are starting points. If your team identifies a different AI opportunity during discovery, you can propose it. All alternative project scopes must be approved by the partner and the instructor.
