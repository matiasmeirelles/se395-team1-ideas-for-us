## What They Do

IDEAS for Us is a UN-accredited nonprofit focused on environmental action. Not advocacy. They turn community ideas into funded, scalable solutions.

- Founded 2008 in a UCF dorm room by Chris Castro
- Clayton Ferrara (Rollins alum, class of 2009, Biology + Environmental Studies) is co-lead
- Operations in ~40 countries
- US offices: Orlando (HQ), St. Petersburg, Miami, Washington DC, San Francisco
- 501(c)(3) nonprofit, tax-exempt
- Revenue through grants, brand partnerships (AT&T, GM, Sprouts), and program funding
- Five sustainability focus areas
- Clayton is a UN delegate

## The Independent Sector

IDEAS for Us sits in the "independent sector" (the overlap between public and private sectors). As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, they are tax-exempt and can access public tax dollars to fund projects. They generate revenue through grants, brand partnerships, and program funding.

## Core Philosophy

30+ years of environmental education told people about problems. IDEAS for Us focuses on the other side: action. Boots on the ground, engineering, financing, and scaling solutions that work.

## Other Programs

- **Fleet Farming:** Urban agriculture program using bicycles, farmlets on front lawns, solar DJ. Hundreds of young people participate.
- **Matuvo Paul (Uganda):** Invented a stacked soil tower for growing food in extreme poverty. Funded by an IDEAS for Us grant. Scaled to thousands of units across the Buduta District.
- **Winter Park Land Trust:** Clayton is a founding member. Raised $3M internally, matched by city for $6M. Became Seven Oaks Park.
